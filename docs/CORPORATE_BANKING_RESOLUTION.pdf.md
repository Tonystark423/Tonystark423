### Section 3: Banking Powers

Each authorized signatory shall have full power and authority, on behalf of the Company, to:

- Deposit, withdraw, and transfer funds
- Sign, endorse, and deliver checks, drafts, and negotiable instruments
- Borrow money and execute promissory notes and loan agreements
- Overdraw accounts and execute overdraft agreements
- Open and close any type of bank account
- Receive statements, notices, and communications
- Execute stop payment orders
- Use all banking services (wire transfers, ACH, credit cards, etc.)
- Appoint agents or attorneys-in-fact
- Execute any documents necessary for banking transactions

### Section 4: Signature Authority

Any ONE (1) authorized signatory may act independently on behalf of the Company. The signature of any one authorized signatory shall be binding and conclusive evidence of authority.

### Section 5: Ratification and Indemnification

All acts performed by authorized signatories in accordance with this resolution are hereby ratified. The Company agrees to indemnify and hold harmless any bank from claims arising from authorized actions.
