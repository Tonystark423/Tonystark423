BANKING POWERS GRANTED:

Each authorized signatory is hereby granted the following powers and authority
on behalf of the Company:

  a. To deposit, withdraw, and transfer funds to, from, and between any accounts;

  b. To sign, make, endorse, accept, execute, and deliver all checks, drafts, 
     notes, bills of exchange, letters of credit, and other negotiable instruments;

  c. To borrow money and to execute, deliver, and perform all promissory notes,
     loan agreements, security agreements, and other evidence of indebtedness;

  d. To overdraw any account of the Company and to execute overdraft agreements;

  e. To open and close accounts of any kind, including checking, savings, money
     market, time deposit, and certificate of deposit accounts;

  f. To receive, endorse, and give receipts for all statements, notices, 
     confirmations, and other communications from any bank;

  g. To execute and deliver stop payment orders and to endorse items for deposit;

  h. To use all banking services, including wire transfers, ACH transactions, 
     safe deposit boxes, credit cards, merchant services, and any other services;

  i. To appoint, remove, or replace any agent, proxy, or attorney-in-fact;

  j. To execute and deliver any and all other documents, agreements, or 
     instruments necessary or convenient to carry out the foregoing powers.
